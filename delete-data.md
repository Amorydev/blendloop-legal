# Delete your Blend Loop data

**Game:** Blend Loop (Android) · **Developer:** AmoryDev · **Contact:** amory.dev@gmail.com

## In the game (fastest)
1. Open Blend Loop and tap the settings button on the home screen.
2. Tap **Privacy**, then **Delete my online data**, and confirm.

This deletes at once:
- your leaderboard profile (nickname, avatar, country, highest level);
- your weekly race entries;
- your anonymous game account and this install's Firebase and analytics ids.

Within 30 days we ask Google Analytics to delete your analytics data (Google removes it from reports within 72 hours and deletes it within about two months) and we delete our BigQuery copy. Crash reports are kept by Google for 90 days and then deleted. Your progress on the device is not touched.

## By email (if you cannot open the game)
Email **amory.dev@gmail.com** with the subject "Delete my data" and your **support code** (Settings → Privacy in the game shows it; tap it to copy the full code, then paste it). Within 30 days we delete the same data as above, ask Google Analytics to delete yours, and reply when it is done.

We cannot find your data from your nickname alone, because nicknames are not unique. Without the support code we can only delete what we can tie to your request.

## What is kept
Nothing that identifies you. Aggregated statistics (for example, how many players finished a level) do not contain your data.

See the full privacy policy: https://amorydev.github.io/blendloop-legal/privacy-policy
